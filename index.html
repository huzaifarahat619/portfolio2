About Me

I'm Huzaifa Rahat, a content creator and social media manager working with brands across Islamabad and beyond. Over the past 4–5 years I've handled the full production cycle — strategy, photography, videography, editing and publishing — for clients spanning jewelry, food, fashion, healthcare and non-profit campaigns. I currently manage content and growth for Safa Jewelers, Reesh Candles Co. and Orange Cream Madina, among others, shooting on professional cameras and iPhone and editing in DaVinci Resolve and CapCut. Before moving fully into content, I completed my MBBS at HITEC-IMS, Taxila (2024) — a background that shaped how I approach research, structure and detail in my creative work. What sets me apart is that I don't hand a project off halfway: I can take a brand from a blank Instagram page to hundreds of thousands of monthly profile visits, because I understand both the creative side and the growth side of social media. I also use AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Veo to speed up ideation and production without cutting corners on quality.

Philosophy

Content that looks good AND performs.

Good content shouldn't just look good — it should perform. Every piece is built around the first 3 seconds, the hook, and a clear reason for someone to stop scrolling, engage and remember the brand.

Process

From idea to analysis.

Idea → Concept → Script/Hook → Shoot → Edit → Publish → Analyze → Improve. I run every project through this cycle, using performance data to make the next piece stronger than the last.
